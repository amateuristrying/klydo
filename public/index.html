Why Klydo

Every AI you use starts from zero. You paste the same context, rewrite the same brief and lose the good idea somewhere around tab forty. Klydo keeps one living memory of your idea and hands it to every tool you open. The council knows what the designer drew. The designer knows what you said on your walk. Your notes know all of it.
